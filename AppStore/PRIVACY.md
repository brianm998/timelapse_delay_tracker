# Privacy Policy: Timelapse Delay Tracker

**Effective date:** 2026 (version 1.0)

## Summary
Timelapse Delay Tracker does not collect any data.

## What the app stores
The move start time and shooting interval you enter are saved on your device only (in the app's local settings, `UserDefaults`) so they are remembered between launches. They are never sent anywhere.

## What the app does not do
- It does not collect personal information, usage data, analytics or diagnostics.
- It does not use the network.
- It contains no third-party SDKs, advertising or trackers.
- It does not require an account.

Deleting the app removes the stored settings.
